I'm Vivek Subramanian — an applied scientist working on LLM post-training:
supervised fine-tuning, distillation, and long-context techniques, with a focus
on getting them to work in production.

This blog is where I write up practitioner's notes: the failure modes, tradeoffs,
and evaluation pitfalls that don't show up in papers but decide whether a
fine-tuning effort actually ships.

Find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/vman049).
